Return-Oriented programming(ROP) is a computer security exploit technique thant allows an attacker to execute code in the presence of security defenses such as executable-space protection and code signing.
Almost all modern systems today have some form of DEP(Data Execution Prevention) to prevent shellcode payloads from being executed. Thus the binary's __TEXT section would be executable but writeable, as the __DATA section is the opposite.
ROP re-using legitimately executable instructions from with the binary's __TEXT section. Use some sequences of instructions(may be named "gadgets"), connect then in a specific order. Repeatedly returning into different parts of the program and executing instructions out of order, carry out a specific task finally.
